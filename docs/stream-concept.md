# The stream

A personal research site laid out as one still page.

A short identity column stays in view: who I am, what I study, and where to find me. Beside it, the selected papers run as a single stream, one after another. Each entry is a title, one sentence on what the paper contributes, a small working demonstration of the paper's core mechanism, and links to the paper. The demonstrations are the only things that move. Everything else, the full list of publications and posters, follows below as a plain list.

A visitor should understand the person and reach a paper without touching anything. The demonstrations add depth for those who do.

Origin: the user's reaction to https://www.beautifului.dev/ (September 2026).
