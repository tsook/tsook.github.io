\cvsection{\I{R}ESEARCH \I{I}NTEREST}
% Rewritten from the one-page research statement, v06 (16 September 2026),
% keeping the sentence structure of the previous CV paragraph.
{\fontsize{11}{14.5}\selectfont\hyphenpenalty=10000\exhyphenpenalty=10000
My work falls in the intersection of \textbf{human-computer interaction (HCI)} and
\textbf{natural language processing (NLP)}, focusing on \textbf{AI that helps people discover
goals worth pursuing}, not just execute them. Specifically, I (1) design \textbf{interactive
systems} that let users explore and compare possibilities to reshape their goals, and
(2) \textbf{model} how goals take shape through interaction to train and evaluate AI that
supports this process.\par}
